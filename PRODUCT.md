# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js + React + Tailwind CSS

## Users
Recruiters, engineering managers, potential clients, collaborators, and peer developers evaluating Santi Subidia's technical expertise, project quality, and professional background.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio and developer showcase for Santi Subidia. It exists to establish professional credibility, present standout engineering and design work, detail career history and technical competencies, and offer clear contact channels.

## Positioning
A distinct, highly polished developer showcase that proves technical execution and high design craft through the quality of the portfolio itself and the depth of its featured projects.

## Operating Context
Evaluated primarily on desktop and mobile web. Visitors typically spend 30 seconds to 3 minutes scanning featured work, live demos, repositories, technical stack proficiencies, and contact information.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Highly responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
- Featured project showcases with descriptions, tech stacks, links (GitHub/live URLs), and key achievements.
- Professional experience timeline, biography, and core competencies.
- Accessible, performant, and clean Next.js + Tailwind architecture.
- Contact / social reach-out links (GitHub, LinkedIn, Email).

## Brand Commitments
- Identity: Santi Subidia
- Voice & Tone: Authentic, technically sharp, articulate, and craft-driven.

## Evidence on Hand
- Repository: santi-subidia
- Further project case studies, work history, and contact links to be provided/curated during build.

## Product Principles
1. **Show, Don't Just Tell:** Prioritize concrete projects, live links, and meaningful technical summaries over generic keyword lists.
2. **Speed & Scannability:** Ensure instant clarity, fast page loads, and effortless navigation for busy recruiters and evaluators.
3. **Craft as Proof:** The portfolio's own UI polish, responsiveness, performance, and attention to detail serve as direct evidence of engineering ability.
